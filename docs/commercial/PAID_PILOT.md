# SentinelAI — Paid Technical Pilot

**Commercial status:** Offer proposal; not a claim of an existing customer, sale, certification, or independently validated benchmark.

## Buyer problem

Teams deploying AI workflows need practical evidence that security controls execute, failures are handled safely, and trace records can be inspected. SentinelAI is positioned as a technical pilot to evaluate those controls in one agreed workflow.

## Proposed pilot

**Proposed fixed fee: US$2,500.** Final price and scope are subject to a written statement of work.

Included:
1. One agreed non-production AI workflow and threat model.
2. Review of the relevant guardrails and failure paths.
3. A bounded test set covering agreed malicious and benign cases.
4. Trace-integrity and evidence-retention review.
5. Reproducible latency and functional measurements in a named environment, if the runnable implementation supports them.
6. A concise findings report: observed results, limitations, and prioritized next steps.
7. A readout delivered as a written report; meetings only if agreed in advance.

Excluded unless separately contracted: production deployment, clinical or legal validation, penetration testing beyond the agreed test plan, formal certification, regulatory legal opinions, guaranteed detection rates, and ongoing support.

## Acceptance criteria

Before work starts, both parties agree in writing on the target workflow, environment, data restrictions, test cases, measurements, deliverables, dates, and payment schedule. Results are reported as measured; untested claims remain unverified. Use synthetic or customer-approved test data only. No production credentials are required for the initial pilot.

## Commercial next step

Request a short written scoping exchange describing the workflow, deployment constraints, and decision the buyer needs to make. The pilot begins only after the statement of work and payment terms are accepted.

## Evidence discipline

Repository architecture is not proof of production readiness. Benchmarks, security properties, privacy behavior, and regulatory mappings must be independently checked against the implementation and the agreed environment. This pilot is not a certification and does not guarantee regulatory compliance.

## IP and licensing

No source-code licence, transfer of ownership, exclusivity, or right to redistribute is included in this offer unless expressly stated in the signed agreement. Customer-specific terms, background IP, deliverable ownership, and permitted reuse must be defined in the statement of work before payment.
